Configure your SMTP credentials so the app can send new member notifications, mass emails, and duty reminders.

Gmail App Password — quickest setup:
1. Go to myaccount.google.com → Security
2. Enable 2-Step Verification if not already on
3. Search for App Passwords
4. Create one named "Champions Connect"
5. Copy the 16-character password
6. Enter your Gmail address + that password below
⚙️ Open Email Settings →

🔐 Single Sign-On (SSO)
Planned for a future release.

SSO will allow members to log in with Google or Microsoft — no password needed. When implemented, members click "Sign in with Google", it matches their email to their member profile, and they're in automatically.

What's needed to enable it:
  • A Google Cloud project (free)
  • OAuth 2.0 credentials from Google
  • A custom domain (e.g. connect.champions.ca)
