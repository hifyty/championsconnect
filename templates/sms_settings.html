📖 How to Set Up Twilio (Free)
  1. Go to twilio.com/try-twilio and create a free account
  2. Verify your phone number during signup
  3. From your Console Dashboard, copy your Account SID and Auth Token
  4. Get a free Twilio phone number: Console → Phone Numbers → Get a Number
  5. Paste all three values below and click Save
💡 Free Twilio accounts can only send SMS to verified numbers. For full sending, upgrade to a paid plan (~$1/month + $0.0075/SMS in Canada).
