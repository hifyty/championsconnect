Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what information Champions Connect collects on behalf of {{ settings.org_name or 'RCCG Champions Parish' }}, how it's used, and your choices. By using the app or website, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

  • Account & profile information: name, email, phone, address, and date of birth if you create an account or are added as a member.
  • Giving records: donation amounts, dates, and designated funds, used to issue tax receipts and maintain financial records.
  • Children's ministry information: a child's name, date of birth, allergy/medical notes, emergency contact, and an approved-pickup guardian list, entered by parents/guardians or staff for safety during check-in and check-out.
  • Participation records: fellowship, choir, event, and attendance information.
  • Questions & prayer requests submitted through the app, including anonymous Q&A during live sessions.
  • Basic usage/audit logs (who did what, when) for security and accountability.

How We Use Your Information

  • To run ministry programs, events, and giving.
  • To issue official donation tax receipts.
  • To keep children safe during check-in and check-out.
  • To send email or SMS updates you'd reasonably expect (announcements, duty reminders).
  • To maintain records we're legally required to keep.

Payment Processing

Online giving is processed by Stripe. Champions Connect never sees or stores your card number — Stripe's secure checkout handles that, and only the resulting donation amount and date are recorded here.

Children's Information

Information about children is entered by a parent/guardian or authorized staff member, not by children themselves. Access is limited to Children's Ministry staff and administrators, and is used solely to keep children safe during drop-off and pickup. We retain it only as long as the child is active in the program, plus a reasonable period after.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your information. It's shared only with the service providers that help us run the app (payment processing, email/SMS delivery, hosting and database providers) and only as needed to provide the service, or when required by law — for example, maintaining donation records for CRA charitable tax receipt purposes.

Data Retention

Donation records are retained as required for tax and charitable-registration purposes. Other personal information is retained while your account or your child's enrollment is active, and for a reasonable period afterward, or until you request deletion as described below.

Your Choices

You can review and update your information from your Member Portal at any time. You can request deletion of your account from Member Portal → Account Settings → Delete My Account. Because charities are required to retain donation records for tax purposes, giving history stays on file even after an account is deleted, but your login access and optional contact details are removed. For any other request — including questions about your child's information — contact us using the details on our Contact Us page.

Security

Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, not plain text. Forms are protected against cross-site request forgery, and login attempts are rate-limited to slow down abuse. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable, standard precautions.

Children's Online Privacy

This app is not directed at children and is not intended for direct use by children. Information about children in our Children's Ministry program is entered and managed by parents, guardians, and authorized staff on the child's behalf.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.
