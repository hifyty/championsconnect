📖 Setup Guide
  1. Go to developer.intuit.com
  2. Create a new app → Select QuickBooks Online
  3. Under Keys & OAuth, copy your Client ID and Client Secret
  4. Add your Redirect URI:
    https://yourapp.onrender.com/quickbooks/callback
  5. Paste credentials above and click Save
  6. Click Connect QuickBooks to authorize
