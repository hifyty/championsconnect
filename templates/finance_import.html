📊 How to Export from QuickBooks
  1. Open QuickBooks Online or Desktop
  2. Go to Reports → Transaction List by Date
  3. Set your date range (e.g. Jan 1 – Dec 31)
  4. Filter by Transaction Type: Sales Receipt or Payment
  5. Click Export → Export to CSV
  6. Upload the downloaded CSV above
✅ What gets imported automatically:
  • Transaction date
  • Customer/donor name (matched to member if email matches)
  • Amount
  • Memo/description as notes
  • Account name matched to donation category
💡 Full QuickBooks API sync (automatic two-way) is on the roadmap for when the app is hosted online. The CSV import is the bridge for now.
